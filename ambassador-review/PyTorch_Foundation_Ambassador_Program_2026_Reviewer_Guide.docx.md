**REVIEWER GUIDE • 2026 PROGRAM CYCLE**

# **About this guide**

Thank you for supporting the PyTorch Foundation Ambassador Program by serving as a reviewer. Your evaluations help identify individuals who have demonstrated meaningful engagement with PyTorch Foundation projects and communities and who have the potential to contribute positively as Ambassadors during the upcoming program year.

Final Ambassador selections are made by the PyTorch Foundation program team. Reviewer scores and comments inform the final selection process.

# **Your role**

You will receive a reviewer workbook containing your assigned applications. Each application will include:

* Submission ID  
* Nominee Name  
* Selected Application Questions and Responses  
* Selected Contextual Information  
* Reviewer Scoring Questions  
* Reviewer Recommendation  
* Reviewer Comments

The reviewer workbook presents selected application questions and the nominee’s responses using the original question wording. Administrative, contact, consent, and eligibility-verification fields may be omitted because they are handled separately by the program team and should not be scored.

| Evidence standardYou are not expected to verify information outside the submitted application unless the nominee has provided supporting links. |
| :---- |

# **Review process**

For each assigned nominee:

1. Read the selected application questions and the nominee’s responses.  
2. Review any supporting links included by the nominee.  
3. Score each review question using the 1–5 scale.  
4. Select a Reviewer Recommendation.  
5. Provide a brief summary explaining your recommendation.

# **Scoring scale**

Use the following rubric for all review questions. Select the score that best reflects the strength, relevance, and clarity of the evidence provided for that specific question—not the nominee’s overall application.

| Score | Rating | How to interpret the score |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | Limited Evidence | Little or no relevant evidence is provided. Claims are vague, unsupported, unrelated to the question, or do not demonstrate meaningful engagement or impact. |
| 2 | Emerging Contributor | Some relevant evidence is provided, but the contribution is early-stage, occasional, limited in scope, or not yet supported by clear outcomes or sustained involvement. |
| 3 | Solid Contributor | Clear and relevant evidence demonstrates meaningful contribution, consistent participation, or credible impact. The nominee meets the expectations represented by the question. |
| 4 | Strong Contributor | Substantial evidence demonstrates sustained contribution, notable impact, leadership, initiative, or strong community value beyond the baseline expectations for the question. |
| 5 | Exceptional Contributor | Compelling and specific evidence demonstrates exceptional depth, breadth, sustained impact, leadership, or influence. The nominee stands out as an exemplary contributor for this question. |

## **Applying the rubric consistently**

* Score each question independently. Strong evidence for one question should not automatically increase scores for other questions.  
* Use whole-number scores from 1 to 5\. Do not leave a question unscored unless you are abstaining because of a conflict of interest.  
* Evaluate the quality of evidence, not the prestige of an employer, institution, job title, follower count, or personal familiarity with the nominee.  
* Consider the relevance, specificity, consistency, duration, reach, and demonstrated outcomes of the work described.  
* Do not infer accomplishments that are not stated or supported in the application or in links supplied by the nominee.  
* Apply the same evidence standard to all nominees. Regional recruitment priorities inform final cohort composition but should not replace evidence-based scoring.

| When evidence is mixedChoose the score that best matches the evidence as a whole and use Reviewer Comments to explain important strengths, gaps, or conflicting signals.  |
| :---- |

# **Score calculation**

Each nominee is evaluated across eight review questions. Each question is scored on a scale of 1–5 using the definitions above. The reviewer score is automatically calculated by summing all eight scores.

| Review Area | Number of Questions | Minimum Area Score | Maximum Area Score |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Foundation Project Engagement | 2 | 2 | 10 |
| Community Impact | 2 | 2 | 10 |
| Ambassador Potential | 2 | 2 | 10 |
| Communication & Advocacy | 2 | 2 | 10 |
| Total | 8 | 8 | 40 |

| Score rangeEach nominee is evaluated across eight questions, and every question receives a score from 1 to 5\. Therefore, the minimum possible total is 8 points (8 questions × 1 point), and the maximum possible total is 40 points (8 questions × 5 points). Each review area contains two questions, so each area score ranges from 2 to 10\.Scores are calculated automatically in the reviewer workbook. Reviewers are responsible only for selecting a score for each review question. |
| :---- |

# **How application responses map to the review questions**

The reviewer workbook presents selected application questions and the nominee’s original responses. The table below identifies the primary application information reviewers should consider for each review area.

| Review Area | Primary Application Information to Consider |
| :---- | :---- |
| Foundation Project Engagement — Questions 1–2 | Projects the nominee is familiar with; how the nominee has contributed to the community; description of contributions to PyTorch Foundation projects and communities; relevant supporting profiles and links |
| Community Impact — Questions 3–4 | Community contribution types; contribution descriptions; evidence of education, mentorship, events, outreach, localization, community leadership, or helping others participate and contribute |
| Ambassador Potential — Questions 5–6 | Why the nominee wants to become an Ambassador; how the nominee proposes to contribute; Ambassador Focus Areas; proposed activities, audiences, outputs, intended outcomes, and measures of success |
| Communication & Advocacy — Questions 7–8 | Contribution descriptions; proposed Ambassador activities; evidence of technical content, talks, workshops, tutorials, documentation, educational resources, community communication, and relevant supporting links |
| Context only — not scored separately | Application type, affiliation or organization, current location, years of community involvement, primary community region, and primary country during the Ambassador term |
| Additional information | Consider wherever it provides relevant evidence for one or more review questions.  |

| ImportantThis mapping is intended as a guide. Information provided in one application response may be relevant to multiple review questions. Reviewers should consider all relevant evidence in the application and supporting links while scoring each question independently. |
| :---- |

# **Review questions**

## **Foundation Project Engagement**

### **Question 1**

Has the nominee demonstrated meaningful engagement with PyTorch Foundation projects or communities?

Examples may include:

* Contributions to PyTorch Foundation projects (e.g., PyTorch, vLLM, DeepSpeed, Ray, Helion, Safetensors)  
* Documentation and educational content  
* Research and technical innovation  
* Community support and mentorship  
* Open source contributions  
* Developer advocacy and outreach

Score: 1–5

### **Question 2**

Has the nominee demonstrated measurable impact through technical contributions, community engagement, documentation, education, mentorship, or advocacy within PyTorch Foundation projects or communities?

Score: 1–5

## **Community Impact**

### **Question 3**

Has the nominee actively supported, mentored, educated, or organized activities for the community?

Score: 1–5

### **Question 4**

Has the nominee helped others learn, participate, contribute, or build community?

Score: 1–5

## **Ambassador Potential**

### **Question 5**

Does the nominee present a clear, realistic, and achievable plan for Ambassador activities?

Score: 1–5

### **Question 6**

Are the proposed activities likely to create meaningful value for PyTorch Foundation projects and communities?

Score: 1–5

## **Communication & Advocacy**

### **Question 7**

Has the nominee demonstrated the ability to communicate technical concepts effectively?

Examples may include:

* Talks  
* Workshops  
* Tutorials  
* Blogs  
* Documentation  
* Educational content

Score: 1–5

### **Question 8**

Does the nominee demonstrate the knowledge, engagement, and communication skills necessary to effectively represent PyTorch Foundation projects and communities as an Ambassador?

Score: 1–5

# **Reviewer recommendation**

Select the recommendation that corresponds with the application’s total score:

| Total score | Recommendation |
| :---- | :---- |
| 32–40 | Strongly Recommend |
| 24–31 | Recommend |
| 16–23 | Borderline |
| 8–15 | Do Not Recommend |
| No scores entered | Abstain (Conflict of Interest) |

| Borderline: The total score is between 16 and 23, indicating some relevant evidence but also notable gaps, limited scope, or insufficient consistency. The application may require further consideration by the program team. |
| :---- |

# **Reviewer comments**

Please provide a brief summary explaining your recommendation. Comments are particularly helpful for:

* Exceptional candidates  
* Borderline candidates  
* Applications with conflicting signals  
* Conflict-of-interest situations

| Completion requirement: Complete every application assigned to you. For each application, enter scores for Questions 1 to 8, select the corresponding reviewer recommendation, and provide a brief reviewer comment. Before the deadline, confirm that the Review Status displays Complete for every assigned application. |
| :---- |

# **Conflict of interest**

If you have a personal, professional, or financial relationship with a nominee that may influence your evaluation:

* Do not score the application.  
* Select “Abstain (Conflict of Interest).”  
* Leave a brief note in the Reviewer Comments field.

# **Eligibility requirements**

The following requirements are verified through the application process and should not be scored separately:

* Minimum age requirement  
* PyTorch Foundation Code of Conduct acknowledgement  
* Linux Foundation Antitrust Policy acknowledgement  
* Application eligibility requirements

# **Review principles**

Reviewers should focus on:

* Evidence provided in the application  
* Demonstrated impact  
* Potential future contribution  
* Community engagement  
* Knowledge of and engagement with the PyTorch Foundation-hosted project(s) the nominee seeks to represent

| Review goalIdentify candidates who can meaningfully contribute to and strengthen PyTorch Foundation projects and communities as Ambassadors. |
